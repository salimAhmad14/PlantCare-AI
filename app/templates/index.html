Step 1

Name it

An EfficientNet-B0 classifier trained on 28,843 grape and tomato leaf images picks one of 13 classes. Below the confidence threshold, or when the leaf does not resemble the training data, it says so and stops instead of guessing.

Step 2

Measure it

The leaf outline is separated from the background by colour, then anything inside it that is not healthy tissue counts as lesion. Lesion area over leaf area gives the severity band.

Step 3

Cite it

The predicted class filters a hybrid dense + BM25 index of the IPM documents. Advice is taken from those passages, with the page number attached.
